## Research digest

**BEAM reality check.** The benchmark's own ICLR 2026 paper ("Beyond a
Million Tokens", arXiv:2510.27246) puts the best published 100K-split
average at **0.358** (LIGHT on Llama-4-Maverick); vanilla/RAG baselines
land 0.24-0.33. Our stand's 32.8 (v10ctl) / 34.5 (v9combined) / 35.3
(B0 record) sit INSIDE the published SOTA band — the carried "leaders at
73-77 vs us at 37" framing does not match the paper and needs one
apples-to-apples check (our judge differs from the paper's nugget
scoring; the harness has `--nugget-judge`). V11 brief item: re-score the
best arm's existing checkpoint with the nugget judge before setting any
"gap to leaders" agenda.

**LIGHT's load-bearing component at 100K is the scratchpad, not
retrieval**: their ablation shows removing retrieval changes NOTHING at
100K (the scratchpad alone suffices), while scratchpad/noise-filter
removal costs 1-2pp; summarization is their largest relative gain
(+160%) — and it is exactly our weakest V10 row class. The scratchpad =
an incrementally-merged salient-state digest (per-turn extraction →
iterative merge → 15K-token compression at a 30K threshold → semantic
chunking + per-chunk binary relevance filter at read time). This is NOT
the write-side value-slot lifecycle that measured negative in V9 — it is
a rolling narrative digest handed to the generator wholesale. Candidate
V11 leg: a derive-time scratchpad surface (per-conversation rolling
digest as its own prompt section under the insight budget). Their other
transferable numbers: retrieval budget K=15 optimal at 100K (+7.4% over
K=5), K=20 degrades; sparse retriever beats dense at ≤1M tokens.
Everyone is weakest on contradiction resolution (LIGHT CR ≈ 0.03-0.05
on their scoring) and strongest on abstention — our 'verifier' arm at
0.85 is competitive with anything published.

**Abstention residual (V11 agenda 2) pricing.** The R2 law says
mini-class prompt engineering is exhausted; the upgrade paths are cheap:
- Stronger audit model on the verifier call (≈3.5K in / 0.25K out per
  question): full-400 BEAM audit ≈ **$3.60 on gpt-4.1** ($2/$8 per M),
  ≈ **$2 on o4-mini** ($1.1/$4.4), ≈ **$0.55 on GPT-5-mini** ($0.25/$2)
  — GPT-5-mini is both cheaper than gpt-4.1-mini's successor pricing
  and a class stronger; the experiment costs single dollars per run.
- Targeted NLI-entailment, zero marginal cost: **Bespoke-MiniCheck-7B**
  (SOTA on the LLM-AggreFact grounded-factuality leaderboard,
  Ollama-able, runs locally) — form the hypothesis as the declarative
  rewrite of query+answer and check it against the evidence bundle;
  MiniCheck-Flan-T5-Large (770M) as the cheap tier. Caveat: claim-level
  support checks pass fabrications assembled from real facts unless the
  CONNECTING claim is itself checked — decompose the answer and include
  the relationship claim as its own hypothesis (the V10 §5 lesson,
  ported to NLI).
Recommendation: V11 runs BOTH arms on the 40-question abstention row —
the strong-judge arm (GPT-5-mini first) and the MiniCheck arm — against
the replicated 'verifier' 0.85 baseline; combined cost under $5.
