## Builds (all default-off, gates green: 236 suites / 1979 tests)

### §1 Day-granular slot supersede + slot cosine gate (0084)

Migration 0084 patches the two `bitemporal_event` validFrom
comparisons — the supersede decision and the backdated guard — to DAY
granularity (`time::floor(_, 1d)`; semantics verified live on
SurrealDB 3.1.5): the v9lifecycle audit showed same-day paraphrases
superseding through the occurred_on `T00:00` vs sessionDate `hh:mm`
asymmetry. Same-day pairs now fall to the score margin (unreachable
for batch rows) and stay COMPETING for the contradiction lane. The
competing pool gains its own cosine gate — `DERIVER_SLOT_SIMILARITY`,
default 0.9, a trailing optional 25th arg (fn::resolve_facts
redefined in lockstep; both TS call sites pass it) — because the
shared 0.85 measured clustering whole topics and cannot be tightened
globally without changing live-ingest 'bitemporal'.

### §2 Update-story rendering (`RETRIEVAL_UPDATE_STORY`)

V9 program law #3: knowledge_update rewards the update STORY, not the
current value — the bitemporal closure hid the old value and made the
row worse. Evidence facts are batch-checked for superseded
predecessors via the reverse supersededBy links (indexed since 0059,
≤3 chain generations, one indexed query per generation), and the
winner's prompt line gains "[previously: <value> — until <date>;
earlier: …]". Prompt-side only: retrieval, ranking and citations
untouched; generator and verifier read the same augmented lines.

### §3 Ordering frame + aspect dedup (`RETRIEVAL_ORDERING_FRAME`)

The v9scan verdict: mechanism fired (40/40 EO predictions changed),
content null — ordering questions ride the enumeration frame, which
fights the golds' exact-N constraint and aspect-label granularity.
When the mention record fired, a dedicated order-of-mention frame
replaces it (short aspect labels, order strictly from the record,
honor the requested N), and near-duplicate aspect mentions collapse
inside the record itself (containment ≥0.7 on informative tokens,
earliest line kept — the first mention is the ordering beat).

### §4 Query-time arc assembly (`RETRIEVAL_INSIGHT_EVIDENCE=query_arc`)

The v9arcs verdict: write-time arcs are coverage-thin by construction
(only fact-dense entities clear the composer floor) and stored topics
are decided blind to the questions. 'query_arc' assembles the insight
slot at read time: the topic phrase extracted from the question
(deterministic scaffold strip, no LLM) scans the ATOMIC fact record
dense+BM25 coverage-first, the shared relevance floor decides
on-topic, the most topical beats (12-beat budget) emit
chronologically as a dated TOPIC RECORD. Same dispatch
(summary/enumeration lanes), same separate budget; fact legs exclude
stored insight rows exactly as under 'routed'.

### §5 Verifier topic-coverage (`RETRIEVAL_VERIFIER_TOPIC_COVERAGE`)

The v9abstain residual: 13/40 misses were fabrications ASSEMBLED from
real facts — each claim individually grounded, the connecting link
invented — and the base audit passes them as 'supported'. The
topic-coverage audit (a) treats asserted connections between facts as
claims needing their own evidence and (b) outputs a
`questionAnswered` judgment; in lenient guardrails under
abstentionCalibration='verifier', supported-but-not-answering
declines with reason low_coverage. Strict/answer guardrails keep
pre-V10 semantics.

## Architecture + algorithm pass (owner ask, same session)

**God-file**: synthesize.service.ts had crept to 916 physical lines
(the 800 max-lines gate survives only via skipComments). Two cuts,
both behavior-neutral (236 suites / 1986 tests, tsc, lint green):
runGenerator → generator-client.ts at the verifier.ts seam (916→762);
resolvePromptFrames in evidence-gates — the single flag→frame mapping
for the three V10 prompt switches that were being computed inline and
drilled through two layers.

**Algorithmic ceilings of the V10 lanes (honest inventory):**

| lane | cost per fired question | bound | scale note |
|---|---|---|---|
| mention-scan (V9+R1) | 1 embed + 2 scans of episode_segment (dense = brute-force cosine ORDER BY, no vector index; BM25 via FT index), cap 400/leg | O(segments) | fine at BEAM (~10² segments/world); at 10⁵+ rows the dense leg needs an HNSW::knn path (SEARCH_HNSW_ENABLED precedent) |
| query_arc | 1 embed + 2 scans of knowledge_fact, cap 200/leg | O(facts) | same brute-force-cosine pattern as insight-leg (deliberate consistency); same HNSW gate before prod promotion |
| update-story | ≤3 indexed queries per answer (fact_superseded_by_idx), chain depth ≤3 | O(supersedes) | `supersededBy IN $w` index usage worth an EXPLAIN check at 10⁶ rows |
| dedupeMentionLines | pairwise containment on ≤60 lines | O(n²·t), n≤60 | bounded by the record cap |
| pickArcBeats / sorts | O(n log n), n≤400 | — | — |

The one real scale gate: the two coverage-first lanes do a linear
dense scan per routed question. Correct at eval scale by design
(coverage was the measured failure), but promotion to default on
large tenants goes through a vector-index leg first.

## R2 — verifier quote-forcing probe: NEGATIVE (program law)

A/B replay over the 13 residual abstention fabrications, fresh
evidence bundles captured via debug traces, two judge classes:

| judge | shipped addendum (qa=true) | quote-forcing (qa=true) |
|---|---|---|
| gpt-4o-mini | 10/13 | 11/13 |
| gpt-4.1-mini | 8/13 | 8/13 |

Quote-forcing (the auditor must output the verbatim evidence span
that resolves the query's SPECIFIC ask, else questionAnswered=false)
does not separate: the judge happily quotes an ADJACENT span
("…trying to prioritize tasks for their sprint 1 using a Trello
board…" as resolving "what specific criteria did I use to
prioritize") and calls it an answer. A stronger judge helps some
(10→8) but stays far from separation, and per-case flips are
unstable across models.

**Program law (extends V9 law #1 to the answer level): on
adjacent-topic benchmarks, answer-absence is not detectable by
prompt engineering of a mini-class verifier — neither
relationship-claim strictness, nor an explicit answers-the-question
judgment, nor verbatim-quote forcing.** The 'verifier' abstention
ceiling stands at ~0.675 until a materially stronger audit model (or
a different signal class, e.g. targeted NLI entailment on the
extracted ask) is priced in. verifierTopicCoverage stays built,
default-off, NOT a default candidate.
